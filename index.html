Pin a point, swing the handle, and the image turns around it like a wheel.

Drag to place a pin and handle, then drag the handle to turn. A new line locks the current turn in, so turns stack.

Rings cut the image into bands around the pin, each turning by its own amount: the further from the pin, the more it turns. Turn rotates the whole area inside the reach. Controls that don't apply to a mode are greyed out.

Gears counter-turns every other band: at 0% all turn the same way, at 50% every other band stays still, at 100% they turn the opposite way.

Wedge taper: with a wedge under 360°, 0% cuts the effect off cleanly at the wedge's edges. Turning it up widens a soft feather across each edge.
